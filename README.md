I am a soil scientist trained in pedology, nutrient cycling, and geospatial analysis, with applied experience translating remote sensing and field diagnostics into practical strategies for soil fertility restoration and climate adaptation. My work bridges laboratory analytics — HPLC, flame photometry, nitrogen distillation — with GIS-driven land-use assessment, aiming to quantify degradation patterns and inform sustainable resource management under global environmental change. I am currently pursuing further specialization through the Erasmus Mundus IMSOGLO programme, having been selected as a KNB Scholar (2026) at Universitas Padjadjaran, Indonesia.

**Research Interests** — Resource Management: sustainable stewardship of soil, water, and land resources within agroecosystems · Global Change: climatic and anthropogenic drivers reshaping soil processes, crop productivity, and food security · Soil Degradation: spatial diagnosis and mitigation of fertility loss through pedometric and geospatial techniques

**Education** — B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, Soil Science, MNS University of Agriculture, Multan, Pakistan (CGPA 3.97/4.0). Coursework spanned soil genesis and morphology, nutrient management in degraded soils, soil-water-environment conservation, and GIS applications in soil survey and land-use planning.
