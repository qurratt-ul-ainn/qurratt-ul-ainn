- 🛰️ Experienced in translating **remote sensing and field diagnostics** into practical strategies for soil fertility restoration and climate adaptation
- 🧪 Laboratory background in **HPLC, Flame Photometry, GC-MS, and Nitrogen Distillation**, paired with **GIS-driven land-use assessment**
- 🌍 Passionate about understanding soil degradation patterns and developing sustainable land management strategies under global environmental change
- 🎓 Selected as a **KNB Scholar (2026)** at **Universitas Padjadjaran, Indonesia**
